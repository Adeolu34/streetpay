StreetPay Privacy Policy

Application: StreetPay (Package: com.streetpayngr.smartapp)

Entity: Street-Friendly Unified Services Limited | Partner: 9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB)

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Street-Friendly Unified Services Limited ("StreetPay", "we", "our", or "us") operates the StreetPay mobile application (Package Name: com.streetpayngr.smartapp). We are committed to protecting user privacy, maintaining data transparency, and adhering to the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), and Google Play Developer Policies.

Funds processing and regulated banking operations are provided in partnership with 9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB), a licensed financial institution.

2. Information We Access and Collect

2.1 Personal Identification & KYC Data

  • Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and Residential Address.
  • Date of Birth, Bank Verification Number (BVN), and National Identity Number (NIN) for statutory KYC verification in compliance with CBN AML/CFT regulations.

2.2 Financial & Transaction Data

  • Virtual account details, wallet balances, transaction records, fund transfers, and bill payments handled through 9PSB.

2.3 Photos, Videos & Media (Android Photo Picker Policy)

Media Handling Statement:
Our app allows users to verify and update their profile by uploading photos or videos. These media files are encrypted on the device before upload to protect user privacy. This operation is infrequent and occurs only at user request.
  • StreetPay uses the Android system Photo Picker for selecting media, meaning the application does not require broad access to your photo library or storage.
  • Photos/images are captured or selected solely for account verification (Tier 2/Tier 3 KYC identity documents, proof of address) or user profile updates.

2.4 Camera Access

  • Camera access is requested strictly when you choose to scan QR codes for merchant payments or take live selfie verification for KYC.

2.5 Location Data

  • Approximate or precise location is accessed only with user consent to prevent fraudulent transactions, comply with regional transaction security, and locate nearby POS/service terminals.

2.6 Device & Diagnostic Information

  • Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, and crash reports to prevent unauthorized logins and maintain app stability.

3. Purpose and Use of Collected Data

  1. Financial Services: Facilitating wallet funding, transfers, bill payments, and merchant checkouts.
  2. Identity Verification: Fulfilling mandatory regulatory KYC requirements required by CBN and licensed banking partners.
  3. Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting user accounts against unauthorized access, identity theft, and suspicious transactions.
  4. Customer Support: Assisting you with inquiries, transaction disputes, and technical support.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • 9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB): Licensed partner bank for wallet ledger management, settlements, and compliance reporting.
  • Licensed Identity Verification Providers: For real-time verification of government-issued credentials against statutory databases.
  • Regulatory & Legal Authorities: CBN, NFIU, or law enforcement bodies when mandated by applicable law.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All communications between the StreetPay app and backend servers utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Sensitive media files and credentials are encrypted on the device before transmission and stored in PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In accordance with financial compliance laws, financial transaction logs are retained for statutory periods (typically 6–7 years).

How to Delete Your Account:
Users can request permanent deletion of their account and non-statutory data at any time by:

  • Visiting the in-app Edit Profile > Delete Account menu, or
  • Submitting a deletion request via email to: privacy@streetpayng.com or support@streetpayng.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
